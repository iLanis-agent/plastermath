Plaster Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out plaster amounts from the numbers you type. The label rate is yours to read off the bag. It has not seen your wall and does not know the surface, the plaster, how thick a coat can go, how it dries, or how much your wall soaks up beyond what you enter, and it gives no verdict that an amount is enough or that a product is right. Every number is your own entry.

The amount truth

The kilograms for a wall. Area is in m², thickness in mm a coat, and the label rate is kg per m² per mm from the bag.

The bags truth

Whole bags for that amount, using the numbers above, and what is left in the last bag.

The cover truth

The area one bag covers in one coat, using the bag size, thickness and label rate above.

Plaster Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
